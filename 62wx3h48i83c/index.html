Rich snippets don’t appear by magic. They are the result of adding structured data to your website’s code. Structured data is a standardized format for providing information about a page and classifying its content. Think of it as a specific vocabulary that you use to talk to search engines in their native language.

This vocabulary is known as Schema.org. Schema.org is a collaborative project by Google, Bing, Yahoo!, and Yandex to create and maintain a common set of schemas for structured data on the internet. When you add this “schema markup” to your web pages, you are essentially translating your human-readable content into a machine-readable format that search engines can easily understand and process.

There are a few different formats for implementing schema markup, but the most common and Google-recommended format is JSON-LD (JavaScript Object Notation for Linked Data). It’s considered the easiest and cleanest method because you can insert it as a single block of script in your page’s HTML, separate from the main body of your content.
